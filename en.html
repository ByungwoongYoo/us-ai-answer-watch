US AI Answer Watch No. 1 · Korean sunscreen · November 2026한국어

Google AI Mode named SKIN1004 in 23 of 29 answers. ChatGPT, via the API, named it in 8 of 30.

We asked Google AI Mode and ChatGPT (OpenAI API with web search) 15 sunscreen questions a US shopper might ask, none naming a brand, on two separate days. The same brand showed up at very different rates depending on which AI answered.

Measured November 24 and 25, 2026 · 15 questions × 2 AI tools × 2 runs · 30 + 29 answers

  • Beauty of Joseon came up most often in both tools (ChatGPT API 24/30, AI Mode 24/29).
  • SKIN1004, Round Lab and Isntree came up in 23/29, 18/29 and 8/29 AI Mode answers, but in only 8/30, 6/30 and 0/30 ChatGPT API answers. Etude went the other way: 6 in the ChatGPT API, 1 in AI Mode.
  • The gaps differ by question. On the mineral sunscreen question, AI Mode did not name Beauty of Joseon on either day.
  • The two tools read different sources. Most ChatGPT API answers cited a brand's own site, while AI Mode leaned on retailers and on Reddit, social media and YouTube.

How to read these numbers

  • ChatGPT was queried through the OpenAI API, not the consumer app. App answers may differ.
  • Google AI Mode was queried from Seoul while signed in, with English and US settings. Results inside the US may differ.
  • We counted only whether a brand name appeared in the answer text. We did not separate recommendations from passing mentions, or record order.
  • On day 1, AI Mode showed only links for the first question (oily skin), so that answer is excluded. That is why AI Mode has 29 answers and the ChatGPT API has 30.
  • AI Mode answers that were read before the page finished loading, or whose record was cut off, were reloaded once the same evening (4 on day 1, 2 on day 2).
  • With only two runs, a 0/2 vs 1/2 vs 2/2 difference on a single question may be chance. This is not a guarantee of visibility or proof of cause.
  • This observation has no affiliation with or sponsorship from any brand. Brand names are used only to identify them.
